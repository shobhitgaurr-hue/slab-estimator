Concrete Curb & Gutter Calculator

This dedicated curb and gutter calculator allows you to compute the exact volume of concrete required for municipal, residential, or commercial curb layouts. Poured curb structures are composite shapes containing both the curb itself (vertical section) and the gutter flag (horizontal section).

To calculate the volume of concrete required for a curb and gutter layout, we calculate the combined cross-sectional area of both components and multiply by the length:

Curb & Gutter Estimation Formula:
Total Area (sq. ft.) = [ (Curb Height × Curb Thickness) + (Gutter Width × Gutter Thickness) ] ÷ 144
Volume (yd³) = [ Total Area (sq. ft.) × Length (ft) ] ÷ 27
